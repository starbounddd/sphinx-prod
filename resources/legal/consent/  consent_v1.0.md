## What This App Is (and Is Not)

This app is designed to help you reflect on your wellbeing and provide general educational information and self-care suggestions.

### This app is NOT:

- A medical or mental health diagnosis
- A replacement for therapy or professional care
- An emergency or crisis service

> If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, please contact local emergency services or a trusted adult or professional right away.

## What Information We Collect

To provide this service, we may collect:

- Basic account information (such as login details)
- General profile information (such as age range and language preference)
- Responses to short wellbeing surveys
- Automatically generated summaries based on those responses

### We do not ask for:

- Medical diagnoses
- Therapy or medication history
- Highly personal identifying details (like your address or real name)

## How Your Survey Responses Are Used

Your survey responses are used to:

- Calculate general wellbeing scores using predefined rules
- Show you plain-language explanations of your results
- Offer general, non-clinical self-care suggestions

Your responses are not used to diagnose or treat any mental health condition.

## Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

This app uses AI to help explain survey results and suggest general wellbeing strategies.

**Important to know:**

- AI does not see your raw identity information
- AI does not diagnose conditions
- AI responses are based only on structured inputs (such as score ranges)
- AI output may not always be perfect and should be treated as informational only

## Safety & Content Limits

If your responses suggest higher levels of stress or difficulty:

- The app may adjust the type of content shown
- The app may encourage you to consider additional support
- The app does not provide crisis counseling or emergency intervention.

## Your Choices & Control

You can:

- Stop using the app at any time
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- Choose not to answer optional questions

Deleting your account will remove or anonymize your stored data in accordance with our data retention policies.

## Privacy & Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to:

- Protect your data
- Limit access to sensitive information
- Store personal and survey data separately

Your data will not be sold and will only be used to operate and improve this service.

## By Continuing, You Acknowledge That:

- You understand this app is not a medical or emergency service
- You are using the app for self-reflection and general information
- You consent to the collection and use of your data as described above
- You understand AI is used to generate informational content
